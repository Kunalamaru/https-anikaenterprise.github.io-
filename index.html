About M/s Anika Enterprise

Dedicated Civil Infrastructure Partner for Mumbai's Arterial & Metro Roads

M/s Anika Enterprise was incorporated in March 2022 under the proprietorship of Mr. Mithun K. Shah, bringing over two and a half decades of hands-on civil engineering leadership in Mumbai's road, metro, and urban infrastructure sector.

We undertake projects for the development of major and minor roads and allied infrastructure on a back-to-back basis — including Cement Concrete (CC) roads, asphalt roads, side strips, storm water drains, minor nallas, footpaths, retaining earth panel walls, multimodal integration facilities, and monsoon maintenance in mastic asphalt.

Core Capabilities

Core Civil Engineering Services

Comprehensive execution of rigid & flexible pavements, drainage networks, retaining structures, and urban multimodal transit corridors.

Core Specialty

Cement Concrete (CC) Roads

Full-depth rigid pavement construction for arterial roads, service roads, slip roads, and C.C. passage side strips — covering GSB, DLC sub-base, M30/M40 PQC, dowel/tie bars, groove cutting, and curing.

Rigid Pavement 01

Asphalt & Bituminous Roads

Development of major junctions and arterial flexible pavements including WMM, DBM, Bituminous Concrete (BC) wearing courses, resurfacing, and heavy-traffic road widening.

Flexible Pavement 02

Storm Water Drains, Nallas & Culverts

Construction of RCC roadside storm water drains, minor nallas, box culverts, kerb channels, and utility chambers designed for high-volume Mumbai monsoon runoff.

Drainage Works 03

Footpaths, Paver Blocks & Multimodal Integration

Metro station multimodal integration facilities including pedestrian footpaths, side strips, bus/auto/taxi bays, heavy-duty paver blocks, landscaping, and urban street furniture.

Urban Transit 04

Retaining Walls, RE Panels & Flyover Approaches

RCC retaining walls, flyover approach ramps in Retaining Earth (RE) panel walls, crash barriers with friction slabs, and precision embankment earthwork.

Structural Civil 05

Monsoon Maintenance (Mastic Asphalt) & Traffic Diversion

Rapid monsoon road maintenance using Mastic Asphalt for pothole prevention & filling along live Metro corridors, paired with safe traffic diversion & work-zone management.
